Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions regarding fees, marketing timelines, photography, and client onboarding.

What are Cornerstone's brokerage and transaction fees?
For residential sales, our standard success commission ranges between 1% to 2% based on mandate exclusivity and property scale, payable strictly upon registry completion. For rental tenancies, our fee equals one month's rent for 11-month or multi-year leave & license agreements. There are no hidden upfront cataloging fees.
How long does it typically take to sell or rent a luxury property?
On average, accurately priced prime residences secure qualified offers within 30 to 60 days of syndicated marketing. Rental homes frequently conclude tenant agreements within 10 to 20 days given strong demand from corporate relocations.
Do you provide professional architectural photography and staging?
Yes. Exclusive mandate properties receive complimentary professional architectural shoot coverage, drone aerial cinematography, and virtual 3D Matterport walkthrough scans handled by our internal media production team.
How do you screen and vet prospective tenants or buyers?
Every applicant undergoes rigorous multi-point verification: KYC identity confirmation, employment and corporate credential checks, past tenancy landlord references, and formal credit reporting before an accompanied inspection is arranged.
What documentation is required before listing a home?
We require standard ownership proof (registered sale deed, share certificate, or allotment letter), property tax receipts, occupancy certificate (OC), and RERA registration details if applicable. Our legal advisory team assists in reviewing all title papers.
Can I cancel or modify my listing agreement at any time?
Yes. We pride ourselves on relationship-driven advisory rather than restrictive lock-ins. You may pause, adjust pricing, or terminate non-exclusive mandates with a simple 14-day written notice with no penalty fees whatsoever.
