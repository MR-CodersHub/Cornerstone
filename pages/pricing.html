Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our listing packages, onboarding timeline, and terms.

Are there any additional brokerage commissions on top of the listing plans?
No. For our self-managed Basic and Featured plans, your one-time listing fee is completely inclusive. You do not owe any brokerage or closing commission to Cornerstone when you conclude a deal directly with an enquiry. For our Premium Marketing plan or full brokerage mandates where our licensed advisors handle negotiations in-person, custom standard success fees are agreed upfront in writing.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major payment avenues via secured digital gateways: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Net Banking across 50+ Indian commercial banks, Credit & Debit cards (Visa, MasterCard, RuPay, Amex), and corporate bank wire transfers (NEFT/RTGS). Instant GST-compliant tax invoices are issued immediately.
What are the service terms and listing guarantees?
All listings are subject to ownership verification and RERA conformity checks to maintain our curated quality standard. Your listing validity begins strictly from the hour the property is publicly published, not from payment time. If your property remains unsold at the end of a Featured or Premium term, you are eligible for an exclusive renewal rate at a 40% discount.
How does the professional photography shoot work?
Upon subscribing to a plan or add-on with photography, our team schedules an appointment at your convenience within 48 to 72 hours. Our architectural photographer visits with professional lighting and wide-angle lenses, spending approximately 60 to 90 minutes capturing all living spaces and society amenities. Digitally edited, high-resolution images are uploaded to your listing within 2 business days.
What is the typical timeline for getting my property published?
Once you submit your property details and imagery through the seller dashboard, our compliance team reviews documentation within 12 to 24 business hours. You receive an email and SMS confirmation the moment your listing is live on our platform and indexed for public search.
Can I cancel or upgrade my listing plan at any time?
Yes! You can upgrade from Basic to Featured or Premium at any point by paying the prorated differential amount directly from your seller dashboard. If your property sells before the plan expiration, you can mark the status as 'Sold' or pause active buyer notifications at any time.
