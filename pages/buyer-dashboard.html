Frequently Asked Questions

How do I schedule or reschedule a site visit?
You can schedule a site visit directly from the "Site Visits" tab or by clicking "Schedule a Visit" on any property. To reschedule, locate your confirmed appointment in the Site Visits table and click "Reschedule" to select a new preferred date and time slot.
What happens after I submit a formal offer?
Once submitted, our licensed brokerage team verifies financing eligibility and presents the offer deed to the property seller. The seller may accept, reject, or present a formal counteroffer within 48 business hours. You will receive an SMS and dashboard notification.
How does the property comparison tool work?
Navigate to your "Saved Properties" tab and select the checkbox on up to 3 listings. Click the "Compare Selected" button in the upper right header to generate an instant side-by-side spec comparison covering carpet area, pricing per sqft, amenities, and furnishings.
Are online offers legally binding on Cornerstone?
In this demo environment, offers represent non-binding letters of intent (LOI). In production, an agreement to sell is legally executed only following physical token disbursement, title deed searches, and notarized registration at the Sub-Registrar's office.
How quickly do agents respond to enquiries?
Our private client relationship managers typically respond within 2 to 4 business hours. You can inspect the status and ongoing correspondence log in your "My Enquiries" section.
