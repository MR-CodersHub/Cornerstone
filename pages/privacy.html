Your Data, Respected

Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to help you find a property and to run this website. Here's exactly what happens with that information.

Last updated: 21 November 2026 Effective: 21 November 2026 Questions: concierge@cornerstone.in

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you give us: name, email, phone number, city preferences, enquiry messages, and details you submit with a property listing.
  • Information collected automatically: pages viewed, search filters used, browser type, device type, and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Preferences: theme (light/dark) and language direction (LTR/RTL) saved in your browser.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, show relevant properties, manage listings and dashboards, improve site performance, and send service updates you have requested. We do not sell your personal information.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

This site does not use advertising trackers. We use browser storage to remember your theme, direction, saved properties, and other dashboard actions so your session feels consistent. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We share information only with: advisors who need it to handle your enquiry; service providers who host or maintain the site under confidentiality obligations; and authorities when legally required. We never share your contact details with unrelated third parties for marketing.

5. Data Retention

We keep enquiry and account information only as long as needed to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes. Listing data may be retained after withdrawal for compliance and record-keeping, then deleted or anonymised.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information, including access controls and encrypted transport where supported. No system is completely secure, so please avoid sharing sensitive financial details over unsecured channels.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and ask us to stop using it for a particular purpose. Write to concierge@cornerstone.in and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

8. Children & Third-Party Sites

Our services are intended for adults and we do not knowingly collect data from children. Links to external websites, including map or payment providers, are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

9. Changes & Contact

We may update this policy as our practices evolve. The latest version will always appear here with its updated date. For questions or requests, use our contact page or email concierge@cornerstone.in.
