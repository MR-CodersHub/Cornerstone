Field Checklists

Quick Expert Tips

Essential rules of thumb compiled by our senior field advisors to safeguard your property decisions.

Before You View a Property

  • ✓ Verify the registered carpet area vs super built-up ratio with the approved layout.
  • ✓ Visit the neighborhood at two distinct times: morning rush hour and late evening.
  • ✓ Check cell network signal strength and mobile internet speeds inside every room.
  • ✓ Inquire about dedicated vs open parking allotment and visitor vehicle parking norms.
  • ✓ Review society monthly maintenance dues and sinking fund status before shortlisting.

Preparing to List Your Property

  • ✓ Complete minor cosmetic paint repairs, fix leaky faucets, and declutter interiors.
  • ✓ Organize original share certificates, property tax receipts, and municipal NOCs.
  • ✓ Schedule photography during peak natural sunlight hours (typically 10 AM to 1 PM).
  • ✓ Set asking price within 5% of recent registered sales in your exact building cluster.
  • ✓ Ensure an authorized representative is readily available for planned weekend viewings.

Understanding Market Timing

  • ✓ Track municipal infrastructure milestones (metro inaugurations, flyover launches).
  • ✓ Monitor RBI benchmark repo rate cycles to lock in advantageous home loan spreads.
  • ✓ Festive quarters (Diwali, New Year) frequently feature builder stamp duty waivers.
  • ✓ For sellers, listed inventory is absorbed quickest during spring transition windows.
  • ✓ Commercial office cycles follow quarterly corporate leasing expansion mandates.
