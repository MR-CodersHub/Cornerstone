Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take for a listing to get approved?
Our listing verification desk reviews all submissions within 24 to 48 business hours. We verify ownership documentation, ensure property photographs meet standards, and validate municipal details to ensure top trust with buyers.
What are the seller commission fees on Cornerstone?
Standard listing on Cornerstone is free with zero upfront charges. Upon a successful closing or lease agreement executed through our network, a transparent 1% advisory brokerage applies for residential sales and 15 days rent for lease agreements.
Can I request professional photography for my property?
Yes! Cornerstone offers complimentary HDR architectural photography and 3D walkthrough scanning for exclusive listings valued above ₹1.5 Cr. Contact your assigned listing agent or submit a support ticket to schedule a photographer shoot.
How do site visits work with prospective buyers?
Buyers request visits through your listing page. You will receive an instant dashboard alert where you can confirm the visit, reschedule to another time, or delegate the accompaniment to your Cornerstone neighborhood agent.
How do I reach my dedicated property advisor?
Every active seller has a designated relationship advisor assigned to their properties (e.g. Priya Sharma or Rahul Joshi). You can call them directly via the phone number listed on your enquiry cards or use the direct inquiry form on this page.
